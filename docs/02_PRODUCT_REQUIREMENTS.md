# Forge AI Platform
## Product Requirements Document (PRD)

**Version**

1.0

## Executive Summary

Forge AI is an AI-native Engineering Intelligence Platform that helps software engineering teams build, understand, maintain, secure, optimize, and evolve complex software systems.

Forge AI is not another AI chatbot.

It is an engineering platform that combines modern backend engineering, software architecture, distributed systems, observability, and AI into one modular system.

The platform assists engineers throughout the complete software development lifecycle while ensuring that every recommendation remains explainable, reviewable, and evidence-based.

## Vision Statement

Build the world's most trusted Engineering Intelligence Platform.

## Mission

Reduce engineering effort while increasing software quality.

## Product Goal

Forge AI should eventually become capable of assisting engineers in:

Understanding unfamiliar codebases.
Reviewing pull requests.
Finding architectural issues.
Detecting performance bottlenecks.
Explaining production incidents.
Improving code quality.
Automating engineering workflows.
Generating engineering knowledge.
Helping teams make better technical decisions.

## Product Philosophy

Forge AI should never behave like a simple chatbot.

Every feature must solve a real engineering problem.

Every recommendation must be explainable.

Every AI decision must be traceable.

Every automation must remain under human control.

## Product Principles

### Principle 1

Engineering before AI.

AI exists only to improve engineering.

### Principle 2

Evidence before opinion.

Never produce engineering recommendations without evidence.

### Principle 3

Human approval first.

Never automatically modify production systems.

### Principle 4

Everything is modular.

Every capability must be independently deployable.

### Principle 5

Enterprise-ready from Day One.

Every module should be designed for:

Security
Scalability
Observability
Maintainability
Extensibility

## Architecture Rule

One final permanent rule:

Every technical decision in Forge AI must answer this question:

"Would a senior engineer at a top product company consider this architecture maintainable, scalable, secure, and production-ready?"

If the answer is no, we redesign it.

## Why this document matters

Most portfolio projects start with code.

We're doing the opposite.

We're first defining:

the mission,
the scope,
the boundaries,
the engineering principles.

That makes every future decision easier and keeps the platform coherent as it grows.

## Before we move to Step 3

I want to make one adjustment to our overall process.

I don't want Forge AI to be just "your resume project." I want it to be something that could realistically become:

a strong portfolio centerpiece,
an open-source project,
something other developers can contribute to,
and potentially even a startup foundation.

So in Step 3, instead of immediately designing the database or folder structure, we'll create a System Architecture Blueprint—the document that defines every major module, how they communicate, and why each exists. That blueprint will guide every line of code we write afterward.
